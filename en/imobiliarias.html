The Problem

Where Agencies Lose Deals Every Day

It is not a lack of leads. It is what happens next that costs you money.

⏱️

A slow reply = a lost lead

The Idealista lead comes in at 10pm. The agent sees it in the morning. By then a competitor has already called. The property could be the same - whoever gets there first closes.

🎯

Agents screening instead of selling

Half the day asking about budgets, property types and whether the person "is really thinking of buying". Time that should go into viewings and negotiations.

🌡️

Warm and cold leads abandoned

Anyone not ready to buy right now gets filed away and forgotten. Three months later they bought - somewhere else. Without nurturing there is no pipeline.

📊

Zero pipeline visibility

Nobody knows where each lead stands. The CRM is out of date, there are no alerts, and the KPIs are worked out by hand at the end of the month.

📅

Manual bookings and slip-ups

Confirmation emails, reschedules and property briefings sent at the last minute. Small details that cost the agency its credibility.

🔁

Inconsistent follow-up

It depends on the agent remembering and feeling up to it. Sometimes they call, sometimes they do not. The result: hot leads that go cold for lack of attention at the right moment.

How It Works

The End-to-End System

From the lead received to the viewing booked - all handled without human intervention. The agent steps in only at the right moment.

The Result

What Changes in Practice

Less screening work. More viewings. More deals closed.

A reply in under 5 minutes, 24/7

Every lead from every channel is contacted within minutes on WhatsApp - at any hour, including weekends and public holidays.

The agent only gets qualified leads

Budget, property type, urgency and buying stage already gathered. The agent joins the conversation with the full picture, ready to close.

No lead is left unattended

Warm leads get email sequences with relevant properties. Cold leads go into a monthly newsletter until they are ready. Zero effort from the team.

Viewings booked automatically

The hot lead books the viewing straight into the agent's calendar, gets the property briefing and an automatic follow-up 2h later.

CRM always up to date, weekly KPIs

Every interaction is logged automatically. Pipeline updated in real time, alerts for inactive leads, and reports with no manual work.

It scales with your volume

Whether it is 10 or 500 leads a month, the system handles them all with the same quality. It grows with the agency without new hires.
