The Problem

Social isolation among the over-40s
has no answer

The WHO Commission recognised it as a global public health priority in 2025. In Portugal, there is no structured solution for adults over 40.

😔

Growing Social Isolation - a Global Public Health Priority

1 in every 6 people worldwide is affected by loneliness - recognised by the WHO Commission in June 2025 as a global public health priority, linked to a higher risk of stroke, depression and premature death. The European Loneliness Survey (JRC / European Commission, 2022) places Portugal among the EU countries with the sharpest rise in loneliness. Divorced people, widows and widowers, parents whose children have left home, relocated professionals: people with a history and with worth, and no tools designed for them.

👥

An Age Group With No Answer

The over-65s have senior universities and day centres. Young people have social networks they grew up with. The over-40s were left out: too young for senior programmes, too old for today's digital dynamics. There are no local, community structures designed specifically for them.

📱

Digital That Isolates Instead of Connecting

Retention algorithms, infinite scroll, vanity metrics. Tinder for twenty-somethings, subscriptions, coins, boosts - a business model built on people's loneliness. Designed to keep you on the sofa, not to get you out of the house.

🗺️

No Data, No Local Response

Data on social isolation in the over-40 age group at municipal level is not systematised in Portugal - it is collected by the municipalities themselves in their Social Diagnosis. The absence of data does not mean the absence of the problem: it means it has not been measured yet.

The Solution

Every feature serves one purpose:
meeting in person

The whole architecture - the delayed photo, identity verification, the mandatory video call - exists to build the trust two people need to meet face to face, safely. Safety and trust are the road to real human contact.

🆓

100% Free, Forever

No subscriptions, no coins, no tricks. Free for every user, now and always. No exceptions.

🏘️

Community by Design

You see who is in your city and neighbouring cities - not random profiles from across the country. Real local communities.

🚫

No Swiping

Intentional browsing, not compulsive. No infinite scroll and no addictive gamification.

📸

Photo After 10 Messages

The photo stays hidden until 10 messages have been exchanged. You get to know the person before judging them on looks.

🔐

Double Identity Verification

Live selfie + proof of address. It removes bots, catfishing and fake profiles.

📹

Mandatory Video Call

Before any in-person meeting, a video call is required. Safety above all.

🔒

A Public and Irrevocable Commitment

The only advertising allowed is non-intrusive ads from local event partners. Never will user data be sold. Never behavioural targeting. Never commercial algorithms. This promise is public and permanent.

🏛️ Institutional Partnership

Municipal and parish councils promote the project among residents aged 40+, help make contact with local cafés and venues, and provide space for meet-ups and community events - at no cost to the public purse. This approach fits the social innovation instruments of Portugal Inovação Social 2030 / PESSOAS 2030, under which municipalities can act as promoters or social investors.
