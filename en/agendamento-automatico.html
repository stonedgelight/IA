Features

Everything You Need to Automate Bookings

A complete toolkit to transform the way clients book appointments and meetings with your company.

24/7 Bookings

Your clients can book at any time, even when you are closed. The system checks availability in real time and confirms instantly.

Automatic Reminders

Confirmations and reminders sent automatically by WhatsApp, SMS or email. Cut no-shows by up to 70% with strategically timed notifications.

Calendar Synchronisation

Integration with Google Calendar, Outlook and other systems. Avoid double bookings and keep the whole team in sync automatically.

Smart Waiting List

When there is a cancellation, the system automatically contacts whoever is on the waiting list, filling the slot effortlessly.

Built-In Payments

Option to take prepayment or a deposit at the moment of booking. Reduce no-shows and secure the client's commitment.

Occupancy Analytics

Detailed reports on occupancy rate, peak hours and no-shows. Optimise your schedule based on real data.
