Features

Everything You Need to Automate Your Email

A complete toolkit to transform the way you communicate by email, cutting time and increasing efficiency.

Smart Automated Replies

Set up automatic replies based on keywords and context. The system identifies the subject of the email and answers with the most suitable information, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Automatic Sorting

Emails organised automatically by category, priority or client. Spend less time hunting for important messages and focus on what really matters.

Scheduled Follow-Ups

Never lose another lead to a missed follow-up. The system sends automatic reminders to you and to your clients, making sure no opportunity slips through.

Gmail/Outlook Sync

Seamless integration with Gmail, Outlook and other email clients. It keeps all your automations in sync in real time, with no complex setup.

Email Analytics

Detailed metrics on open rates, response time and communication effectiveness. Use real data to keep improving your email strategy.

Spam Protection

Advanced filters that protect your inbox from spam and malicious email, keeping your productivity and digital security intact.
